## State

The programme is in **phase 2: external interrogation and compositional scaling**.

Experiment 12 reproduces the published *Growing Neural Cellular Automata* lizard system with fixed upstream weights, then applies project-specific matched interventions. Its **white-box intervention map is frozen**; do not add another NCA sweep by default.

The detailed seven-result causal map, exact numbers, protocols, and limits live in [Experiment 12](../experiments/12-growing-nca/README.md) and the cross-cutting interpretation lives in [Findings](findings.md). The three transfer predictions worth carrying forward are:

1. matched nominal/visible damage can separate by geometry or regional context;
2. internally inconsistent latent/state assignment can be worse than complete local deletion;
3. temporary restriction of corrective action can leave persistent divergence after the restriction is removed.

These are predictions to test in another independently authored system, not universal claims.
