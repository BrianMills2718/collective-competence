# The Crusoe Construction — a cumulative non-biological competence ladder

[Research questions](../questions.md) · [Current work](../current.md) · [Research landscape](research-landscape.md) · [Medium.com article draft](crusoe-construction-medium-draft.md)

## Goal

Create one **cumulative, non-biological worked construction** showing how progressively richer goal-relative competence can be built from simple dynamical components without introducing “intelligence” as a primitive.

The motivating device is analogous to Robinson Crusoe in economics: begin with the smallest intelligible world, introduce one new capability or interaction only when the previous system cannot solve a specified challenge, and make the consequence of each addition explicit. “Friday” marks the transition from an isolated competent system to coordination, exchange, specialization, and genuinely collective capability.

This is a **constructive explanatory goal and communication artifact**, not a claim that the individual mechanisms are novel. The September 2026 prior-art surveys show that regulation, memory, adaptation, cellular competency, goal scaling, collective morphogenesis, distributed signaling, regeneration, and related mechanisms already have substantial literatures and executable examples. The value of the Crusoe Construction is the cumulative derivation: one transparent artificial world in which the causal provenance of every added capability remains visible.

It is **not the current experimental priority**. Phase 2 remains external interrogation on Growing NCA and related independently authored systems. The Crusoe Construction is a future constructive deliverable to be developed when the project is ready to build the cumulative synthetic lineage.

## Core question

> **What is the smallest non-biological dynamical world in which increasingly rich forms of goal-relative competence can be constructed step by step, while every transition is justified by a challenge the preceding system cannot solve?**

The stronger constraint is:

> **Never add “intelligence.” Add only explicit state, sensing, action, memory, communication, plasticity, resources, or organization, and demonstrate what new challenge becomes solvable.**

## Why one world rather than a sequence of unrelated toys

The explanatory point is cumulative. Sorting, thermostats, transport, cellular automata, and regenerative models can each calibrate a distinction, but changing worlds at every step obscures what was added. The Crusoe Construction should therefore use one minimal artificial-material world and extend it conservatively.

A candidate base world would contain only enough structure to support later experiments:

- local sites or particles with finite state;
- local interaction/bond rules;
- one or more conserved or replenished resources;
- explicit costs for state change/action;
- local sensing when introduced;
- local actuation when introduced;
- persistent internal state when introduced;
- communication channels only when introduced;
- no biological vocabulary required by the substrate itself.

The reusable scientific object remains trajectories, interventions, matched comparisons, and evidence—not a universal simulator architecture.

## Proposed ladder

The exact rung count is provisional. Every rung must earn its place experimentally.

| Rung | New ingredient | Capability to demonstrate | Required contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Matter** | local state + local dynamics | pattern formation / passive convergence | no competence claim; establish attractors and constraints |
| **1 — Persistence** | bounded organization + resource accounting | remain inside a viability region | compare with an equally ordered but non-restoring structure |
| **2 — Crusoe** | sensing + action + feedback | actively restore a variable after disturbance | passive relaxer or open-loop control |
| **3 — Memory** | persistent internal state | choose differently in observation-identical states with different histories | prove/show memoryless insufficiency first |
| **4 — Learning** | plastic policy/state update | improve or retune performance across recurring environments | fixed-policy and reset controls |
| **5 — Friday** | second bounded competent system + minimal interaction | coordination / mutual compensation | isolated systems and communication-disabled pair |
| **6 — Specialization** | heterogeneous efficiencies/capabilities | expand joint reachable/viable region through division of labor | matched homogeneous pair |
| **7 — Distributed state** | information held across members | solve a challenge no individual observation supports | central-information and no-communication controls |
| **8 — Collective repair** | redundancy/reconfiguration/generative capacity | restore organization after member/component loss | same structure without replacement/reconfiguration |
| **9 — Collective learning** | plastic interaction topology or shared policy | organization-level adaptation | member-only plasticity and frozen-topology controls |
| **10 — Higher-level causal unit** | persistent integrated sensing/action boundary | collective becomes a useful intervention/control scale | test whether macro intervention/prediction adds anything beyond component description |

This table is a design hypothesis, not a commitment to implement every rung. A rung should be deleted, merged, or reordered if the required distinction is not scientifically clean.

## Construction rule: necessity before addition

Each new capability must answer an explicit impossibility, ambiguity, or performance boundary in the previous rung.

Examples:

- **Memory:** first construct two histories with the same current observation but different required actions. If a memoryless controller cannot distinguish them, persistent state has a reason to exist.
- **Communication:** first construct a challenge whose relevant information is distributed so no individual can solve it from local observation alone.
- **Plasticity:** first construct recurring environments where no one fixed policy performs adequately across the family.
- **Generative capacity:** first remove a needed component/type completely and show that correct state information alone cannot make an unreachable state reachable.
- **Collective organization:** first show that the coupled system's competence region exceeds what isolated members or a trivial union can achieve.

Where a limitation can be shown analytically or by an information/reachability argument, prefer that to merely observing failure in simulation.

## What would make the construction scientifically useful

A successful ladder should make several things simultaneously visible:

1. **Continuity:** no unexplained jump from non-agentic dynamics to an “intelligent agent.”
2. **Causal provenance:** each competence is tied to an experimentally manipulated capability or interaction.
3. **Challenge relativity:** competence is always defined relative to a criterion and challenge family.
4. **Costs and bounds:** richer competence is not magic; it consumes information, action capacity, resources, or organizational structure.
5. **Collective transition:** “Friday” and later rungs show exactly what interaction adds beyond two isolated competent units.
6. **Failure:** every rung has conditions under which the competence disappears.
7. **Blind audit:** selected rungs can later be handed to Goal/Competence Discovery with semantics withheld to test whether the analytic arm recovers only what behavior supports.

## Relationship to the external-interrogation programme

The project now has two complementary evidence routes:

- **External interrogation:** use independently authored systems such as Growing NCA, Cellnition/RNM, MinimalDevelopmentalComputation, LENIA Umwelt, bioelectric systems, and biological models to test whether intervention distinctions predict non-obvious behavior outside our own constructions.
- **Crusoe Construction:** use one deliberately transparent lineage where every capability addition and causal dependency is known by construction.

The external route protects against designing systems to validate our vocabulary. The Crusoe route supplies an intelligible, cumulative demonstration and a source of controlled positive/negative cases. Neither substitutes for the other.

## Medium.com communication deliverable

The construction should also be communicated as a public-facing illustrated **Medium.com essay**, provisionally titled **“The Crusoe Construction: Building Non-Biological Competence from the Atoms Up.”** The article is not merely a technical write-up; it is part of the goal because the cumulative argument depends on making every added capability visually legible.

The working article draft, publication structure, and reusable visual grammar live in [crusoe-construction-medium-draft.md](crusoe-construction-medium-draft.md).

The article should make one careful claim:

> **Increasingly sophisticated competent behavior can be constructed from simpler dynamical capacities without inserting intelligence as a primitive.**

It should **not** claim that literal atoms are proven intelligent, that the listed mechanisms are novel, or that the ladder establishes a universal scale of intelligence. “Intelligence goes all the way down” is motivating philosophy; the worked construction is the empirical/engineering demonstration that competence can be built continuously from low-level dynamics.

## Completion condition

This goal is complete when there is:

- one cumulative executable world rather than a collage of unrelated examples;
- a documented sequence of at least several qualitatively distinct capability additions;
- matched controls at each transition;
- at least one analytically or experimentally demonstrated necessity result before a capability is added;
- explicit cost/failure boundaries;
- at least one genuine collective transition after the Friday step;
- a polished Medium.com article with a coherent visual language suitable for a general technical audience;
- no novelty claim that conflicts with the project's prior-art surveys.
