# Scientific Hypergraph extraction

The general Scientific Hypergraph metamodel/IR has been extracted to its own repository:

- **Authority:** `BrianMills2718/scientific-hypergraph`
- **Repository:** https://github.com/BrianMills2718/scientific-hypergraph
- **Default branch:** `main`

The `docs/scientific-metamodel` branch and PR #79 retain the development history and original Collective Competence proving-ground material. Files remaining here should be treated as historical/extraction provenance unless explicitly identified as a Collective Competence downstream model.

Collective Competence is now a **consumer/proving ground** of Scientific Hypergraph, not the authority for the general IR.

Future general kernel/schema/query/validator/viewer work belongs in `scientific-hypergraph`. Future CC-specific theory, experiments, evidence, and generated CC graph instances remain in this repository.
