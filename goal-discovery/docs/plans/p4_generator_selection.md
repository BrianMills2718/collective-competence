# Phase 4 installed-generator selection

## Decision

Stop the current Slime line after P3-006. Select the installed, standard
NetLogo **Heatbugs** model for one thin bridge calibration before attempting a
blind heterogeneous-target inference experiment.

## Why this is the smallest useful move

The missing capability is no longer visualization, movement, or collective
interaction. It is a target-bearing internal state that can be hidden from the
analysis boundary and inferred from behavior.

| Installed candidate | Useful new capability | Primary limitation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heatbugs | persistent identities, individual ideal temperatures, motion, shared field, built-in deep freeze | micro targets are explicitly authored in the standard generator | **thin bridge now** |
| Food Reward Learning | learned internal values and heterogeneous learning rates | prescribed one-way motion and weak collective coupling | defer |
| Slime Mold Network | dynamic network, food signal, particle state, path adaptation | much larger state and measurement problem before a target boundary exists | richer follow-on |
| Muscle Development | cell state and diffusing hormones | hard clamps and scripted exercise physiology repeat controller calibration | reject for this phase |

Heatbugs is not evidence of an emergent collective goal. It is an off-the-shelf
calibration system for testing whether the laboratory can recover heterogeneous
hidden targets from observable motion and field trajectories, then predict a
new perturbation. Its source remains unmodified and its standard NetLogo view
remains the visualizer.

## Allocation

1. P4-001: at most one paired whole-field freeze spike. Require a clear shock,
   recovery, complete per-tick output, and the installed GUI model.
2. P4-002 is unlocked only if P4-001 passes. Hide `ideal-temp`, `unhappiness`,
   and the decision rule from analysis; infer target constraints from observed
   positions, local temperatures, and moves.
3. Stop if the observable trace cannot identify per-agent target information
   better than population and field-only nulls. Do not build a new simulator or
   visualization layer.
