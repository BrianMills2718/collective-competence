# Documentation and research knowledge

- Start at `../../roadmap/README.md`; all durable project knowledge must be
  reachable through that wiki. Native files remain the evidence/authority owners.
- Update the existing concern owner. Topic synthesis explains conclusions,
  counterevidence, uncertainty, and decision implications—not just filenames.
- A current plan contains current gaps, next decisions, and acceptance criteria.
  Completed execution detail belongs in its existing evidence record or history.
- Preserve preregistrations, result measurements, original briefs, and historic
  audits. Historical "next" instructions do not become current assignments.
- Experiment metadata represents one experiment with multiple linked artifacts;
  disclose provisional classifications and unreviewed evidence. Never infer a
  scientific outcome from a filename or generate conclusions from inventory alone.
- After a change, repair wiki routes and source relationships. Link validation
  proves reachability only; independently review goal alignment and claim fidelity.
- Keep shared policy linked, not copied. Physical archiving follows the shared
  lifecycle procedure; do not move records simply to reduce file counts.
