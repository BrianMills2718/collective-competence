# Documentation and research knowledge

- Start at `../../wiki/index.md`; all durable project knowledge must be reachable through that wiki. Native files remain the evidence/authority owners.
- `../../wiki/ontology.md` owns detailed live terminology and conceptual relationships; `PROJECT.md` owns purpose, scope, and scientific boundaries. Plans, results, and wiki topics link to those owners rather than redefining goals, capabilities, competence, robustness, adaptation, mechanisms, or the research-purpose axes.
- Update the existing concern owner. Topic synthesis explains conclusions, counterevidence, uncertainty, and decision implications—not just filenames.
- `../../wiki/current.md` is the derived handoff projection. Accepted top-level plans under `../../docs/plans/` own bounded future-work authorization; completed execution detail belongs in its native evidence record or history. Do not create a competing current-plan surface.
- Use `../../wiki/development-log.md` only when chronology itself matters. Do not make ordinary work depend on reading it, and do not retain a superseded current-state narrative merely as history; promote durable content, then rely on Git/reference history.
- Preserve preregistrations, result measurements, original briefs, and historic audits. Historical "next" instructions do not become current assignments.
- Experiment metadata represents one experiment with multiple linked artifacts; disclose provisional classifications and unreviewed evidence. Never infer a scientific outcome from a filename or generate conclusions from inventory alone.
- After a change, repair wiki routes and source relationships. Link validation proves reachability only; independently review goal alignment and claim fidelity.
- Keep shared policy linked, not copied. Physical archiving follows the shared lifecycle procedure; do not move records simply to reduce file counts.
