## Pilot measures

- **Task-to-goal traceability:** 3/3 sprints named and tested a maturity exit
  question.
- **Scope drift:** no exploratory branch silently became an implementation
  programme. Each sprint ended in its frozen stop branch.
- **Placeholders converted:** the V3 transfer and V4 scale/control placeholders
  became sourced no-go decisions. V5 was retired by its prerequisite rule.
- **Interface assumptions revised:** a generic selector, an organized-looking
  trail scale, and an aggregate causal improvement were all prevented from
  filling mature-interface boxes.
- **Exploration:** the method did not suppress it. P7-004 surveyed candidate
  systems, and P7-005 changed from prediction to causal action value while
  remaining bounded.
- **Box-filling incentive:** resisted in 3/3 sprints. Negative evidence revised
  or closed versions rather than manufacturing positive panels.
- **Planning overhead and decision-time precision:** insufficiently measured.
  Two sprints report bounded decision time, but none independently logged total
  planning minutes. No planning-efficiency ratio is claimed.

## Retain, revise, stop

Retain the project-local loop because it improved claim boundaries, preserved
abstention, and stopped three low-value continuation paths. Revise it before
reuse:

1. log sprint start, planning complete, first evidence, and decision timestamps;
2. let a maturity version close negatively or retire explicitly;
3. distinguish task-specific, retrospective, prospective, and general evidence;
4. record recovery when an implementation fault occurs after a frozen table;
5. treat the proof-of-success artifact as revisable evidence scaffolding, not a
   delivery checklist.

Stop company-wide rollout now. The evidence is three adjacent research sprints
in one programme, planning overhead is incompletely measured, and no non-research
transfer has tested whether the method improves a report, benchmark, customer
journey, API example, or runbook. A future company decision requires one bounded
cross-context pilot with the revised timestamp instrumentation.
