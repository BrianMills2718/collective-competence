## What V1 establishes

The instrument can distinguish passive convergence, regulation, compensation,
and adaptation using matched mechanism nulls. It can infer one hidden scalar
defended target from a deliberately small candidate family.

## What V1 does not establish

It does not yet show that a compact goal model predicts unseen trajectories
better than non-goal alternatives. It does not establish unrestricted goal
discovery, agency, multiscale causal emergence, or general intelligence. That
unresolved model-comparison question is the first Phase 2 gate.
