## What changed

P3-006 prevented a causal reconstruction result from being mislabeled as a
defended target: Slime corrected neither over-compression nor dispersal toward
one stable band.

The next installed model supplied the missing calibration capability. Heatbugs
has persistent identities, individual hidden target temperatures, movement,
and a shared environmental field. P4-001 established a reproducible freeze and
recovery path. P4-002 then recovered useful per-agent targets from movement
inequalities without exposing the target or unhappiness fields to inference.

The key methodological result is compact: controlled counterfactual probes can
turn trajectories into bounds on hidden heterogeneous targets. No fitted model,
custom simulator, or new visualization layer was required.

## Interpretation audit

The target inference is real but calibrated. Heatbugs explicitly assigns every
micro target and directly branches behavior around it. More probes, tighter
grids, or more seeds would improve resolution without changing the scientific
claim. Stop this line now.

The remaining long-term gap is endogenous collective target formation: a
system with internal transient state and distributed interaction must reveal a
useful target-like representation that was not placed in every agent by the
generator author.

## Allocation decision
