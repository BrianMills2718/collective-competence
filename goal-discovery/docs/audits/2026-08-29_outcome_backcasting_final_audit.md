## Scientific-integrity audit

- P7-002's 8.4% result remains a failed 10% prospective gate; its stronger
  ablation remains diagnostic.
- P7-003's opened-data audit does not become prospective evidence.
- P7-004 displays the task-matched null win, all held-seed failures, and the
  corrected intervention boundary without changing any trajectory or score.
- P7-005 displays both the 15.7% pooled benefit and the failed seed/budget/split
  robustness gates. It is retrospective causal evidence, not general control.
- Heatbugs authored truth is joined only after black-box inference.
- V5 is retired instead of manufacturing a competence experiment after V4
  failed to promote a representation.

## Reuse and architecture audit

The work surveys and reuses the installed off-the-shelf stack: unmodified
NetLogo models generate trajectories; Panel, Bokeh, and Tabulator provide the
desktop interface; pandas and existing result tables provide thin adapters.
V6 extracts only the boundary repeated four times—question, generator, maturity,
decision, claim limit, sources, and next decision. It does not create a simulator,
database, dashboard framework, or generic trajectory platform.

## UI comprehension and accessibility audit

At 1280px the live page had `scrollWidth == innerWidth == 1280`. The Outcome
view exposes the north star, claim, evidence state, uncertainty, and next decision.
The V4 view exposes its no-go before the plot and includes tables for every seed
and integrity gate. The V6 control was used to filter four contracts down to the
two Virus cases and restored successfully. Questions, decisions, provenance,
claim limits, and next steps are readable text and tables rather than color- or
hover-only content. Per user direction, desktop is the only acceptance target;
no further phone-specific work is required.

## Planning-method and company audit

Outcome backcasting retained task-to-goal traceability and supported three
bounded no-go decisions without converting interface completion into scientific
progress. It did not measure total planning time well enough to justify broad
rollout. The method is retained with four revisions inside goal-discovery and
stopped at company scope until a measured non-research transfer pilot exists.
