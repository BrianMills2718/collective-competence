# OB-001 pilot review — sprint 2 / P7-004

## Traceability

P7-004 remained directly traceable to the V4 question: whether a higher-level
description adds predictive value after perturbation. The outcome map prevented
the off-the-shelf Ants visual from being treated as progress by itself and
required the trail representation to beat task-matched nulls.

## Artifact effect

The mature-outcome artifact clarified the scientific objective in two useful
ways:

- it forced a single scale/control uncertainty rather than another generic
  family tournament;
- it kept `scale_control` hypothetical after the no-go instead of rewarding a
  completed UI section or an organized-looking pheromone field.

It did not suppress the exploratory reuse check: Flocking and Virus were
considered and rejected because their outcomes were already open, while a fresh
installed Ants generator was selected.

## Cost and decision speed

- Planning overhead was not independently instrumented; do not invent a ratio.
- The frozen production batch completed in under one minute of wall time after
  the specialized adapter was ready.
- The first and final eight-seed evidence arrived in the same batch, inside the
  25-minute and 90-minute caps.
- One analysis-boundary correction was required: tick 301 includes diffusion,
  whereas instantaneous removal is established by the frozen intervention
  command. No trajectory was regenerated and no scientific score changed.

## Learning and avoided work

- All nine integrity gates passed.
- The primary null beat all three organizational scales; trail MAE was 27.032
  versus 17.569 for the null.
- The trail scale won only 3/8 seeds against either alternative scale.
- The frozen branch retires Ants for this predictive question and avoids model,
  threshold, checkpoint, feature, and UI tuning.
- No outcome-map placeholder became measured. The V4 scale/control placeholder
  remains hypothetical, now with a sourced contradiction attached to this
  candidate route.

## Pilot disposition after two sprints

Continue OB-001 unchanged for one final bounded learning sprint. So far it has
improved task-to-goal traceability and made two no-go decisions cheap without
creating pressure to fill a UI. The third sprint should test whether it also
helps choose a qualitatively different route—likely a causal intervention-value
question—rather than merely documenting another predictive no-go. Retain,
revise, or stop the method only after that third sprint as preregistered.
