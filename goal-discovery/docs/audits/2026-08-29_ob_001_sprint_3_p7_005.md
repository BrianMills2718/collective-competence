## Sprint observation

P7-005 remained directly traceable to the mature V4 question: whether a
higher-level description changes intervention value. Backcasting shifted the
task away from another prediction score and toward a matched action comparison.
The cheapest valid evidence was already in the repository, so the sprint reused
stored off-the-shelf Virus trajectories and generated no new outcomes.

The artifact clarified the objective rather than distorting it:

- the pooled 15.7% benefit was not allowed to fill the V4 box because seed,
  budget, and split robustness failed;
- the 2/4 sign-consistency result remained visible instead of being hidden by an
  average;
- a negative result legitimately revised the maturity ladder by closing the
  current internal V4 route rather than creating another implementation task.

## Time and allocation

## Three-sprint pilot disposition

**Retain with revision inside goal-discovery. Do not generalize into company
planning yet.** Across P7-003–P7-005, outcome backcasting kept each sprint tied
to a mature decision, allowed three inexpensive negative results to stop work,
and resisted pressure to fill UI sections without evidence.

Revise the project-local method before any broader proposal:

1. instrument planning time, time to first evidence, and time to decision rather
   than reconstructing them afterward;
2. state explicitly that a maturity version can close negatively instead of
   becoming visually complete;
3. distinguish task-specific measured evidence from prospective/general value
   in version status; and
4. require recovery notes when implementation faults occur after a frozen table
   exists, preserving the original data and gate.

The company-generalization lock remains in force. A separate review must decide
whether these observed benefits and revisions transfer beyond research projects.
