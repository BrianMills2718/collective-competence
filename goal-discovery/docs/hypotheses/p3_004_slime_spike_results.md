**Decision: adopt Slime for one formal causal experiment.** All frozen
integrity and scientific criteria passed.

- Mean baseline aggregation before dispersal was 28.223 nearby cells.
- Deterministic dispersal produced a mean matched gap of 27.328 nearby cells.
- Three of four seeds closed more than half that gap by ticks 570–600; mean
  closure was 56.8%.
- Population, paired pre-branch trajectories, installed source, and the
  unmodified-generator boundary all passed.

This establishes a strong, visible loss-and-recovery trajectory, not a goal
claim. P3-005 must determine whether recovery depends on agents sensing the
chemical field rather than passive evolution alone.
