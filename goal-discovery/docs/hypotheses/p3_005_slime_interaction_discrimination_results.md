# P3-005 — Slime interaction-dependent recovery results

**Decision: promote `interaction-dependent aggregation recovery` as a Phase 3
regulation candidate.** All eight frozen criteria passed.

- With sensing active, late aggregation averaged 24.104 nearby cells.
- With sensing disabled, late aggregation averaged 1.743 nearby cells.
- The mean matched active advantage was 22.362 nearby cells, and every seed
  exceeded the frozen 10-cell threshold.
- The disabled arm regenerated the chemical field in every seed. The result is
  therefore not explained by eliminating the field, population, elapsed time,
  or the random movement stream; access to the field gradient is the decisive
  difference.

This is the strongest unscripted collective result in the repository so far.
It identifies distributed, interaction-dependent reconstruction after loss of
both spatial and field organization. It does **not** yet establish an internal
target representation, an evolved goal, bidirectional error correction, or
generality beyond this model.
