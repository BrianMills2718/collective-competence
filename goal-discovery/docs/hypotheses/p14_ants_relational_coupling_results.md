# P14 — relational Ants proposal abstains before intervention

[Wiki](../../../wiki/index.md) · [Frozen protocol](p14_ants_relational_coupling.md) ·
[Candidate and observations](../../results/p14-ants-relational-coupling/) ·
[Current plan](../plans/current_research_plan.md)

## Decision

Stop P14 before intervention. The frozen `role_relational` family improved on
the simpler families, but it did not clear the precommitted held-seed effect-size
gates. No erasure experiment, causal result, competency, goal, or agency claim is
licensed.

This is a useful abstention rather than a failed implementation. The laboratory
observed one existing interacting substrate, compared a modest fixed proposal
grammar on independent runs, and declined to spend a causal experiment on a
relation that was not distinct enough from the simpler shared-field account.

## Frozen selection evidence

The clean run at source revision `1425a1e` produced 50,000 learner-visible rows
from seeds 7101–7108 and ticks 251–300. Food, nest, source, collection, task
labels, intervention identity, and model rules remained withheld.

| Supplied family | Mean held-seed cosine loss | Role-relational improvement |
|---|---:|---:|
| Persistence | 0.112376 | 12.0% |
| Radial geometry | 0.111892 | 11.7% |
| Shared field | 0.100947 | 2.08% |
| Role relational | 0.098849 | — |

Role relational won 6/8 held seeds against persistence, 6/8 against radial
geometry, and 8/8 against shared field. The win-count gates passed. The frozen
effect gates did not: improvement had to be at least 15% over persistence and
5% over each single-relation family. The observed gains were 12.0% and 2.08%
over the two relevant comparators. The candidate therefore returned `abstain`
before band selection, prediction generation, or intervention.

## Interpretation and alternatives

- **Shared local field relation:** the main descriptive gain came from adding
  the local chemical vector. This is consistent with agent–field coupling, but
  that variable and family were supplied and no causal challenge was earned.
- **Opaque role-conditioned relation:** mode interactions added a small,
  consistent improvement, but not enough to distinguish a role-specific account
  under the frozen standard.
- **Radial geometry:** added almost nothing beyond persistence and is not a
  competitive explanation for these one-step motions.
- **Unexpected goal or competency:** not tested. No task outcome, recovery
  criterion, or compensatory response crossed the learner boundary.

Do not lower thresholds, add nonlinear families, choose a favorable radial band,
or inspect intervention outcomes post hoc. Those moves would convert a valid
prospective abstention into retrospective fitting.

## What changes next

P14 confirms that the candidate/freeze/abstain seam works on an interacting
off-the-shelf model, including the decision not to intervene. It also sharpens
the programme bottleneck: supplying the observable vocabulary and a short list
of relation families still does most of the interpretive work.

Stop the Ants lane. The next design checkpoint should improve **proposal
generation and observable selection**, first against archived systems where
truth and failure modes are already known, before paying for another prospective
substrate. Its success criterion must be useful held-system proposals or honest
abstention without task labels—not another polished viewer or a larger fixed
family menu.
