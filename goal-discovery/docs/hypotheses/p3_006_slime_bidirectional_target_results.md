# P3-006 — Slime bidirectional target discrimination results

**Decision: stop target/setpoint language for the Slime aggregation
representation.** The frozen promotion gate failed cleanly.

- All four new-seed triplets were complete, identical before the branch, and
  preserved all 400 agents.
- Only two of four baselines met the frozen slope requirement for a stable
  candidate band.
- Both interventions were large enough, but no dispersed or compressed seed
  returned to its pre-branch band.
- Late mean aggregation was 21.950 after dispersal and 227.997 after
  compression, versus a mean candidate center of 41.134.

The result classifies the P3-005 phenomenon as **interaction-dependent
attractor reconstruction**, not stable bidirectional target regulation.
P3-005 remains valid causal evidence that field sensing reconstructs
aggregation after damage. P3-006 prevents that result from being promoted
beyond what it shows.
