# Research landscape: what our experiments have taught us

[Project wiki](README.md) · [Experiment register](experiments.md) ·
[Current priorities](../goal-discovery/docs/plans/current_research_plan.md) ·
[Scientific charter](../goal-discovery/docs/PROJECT.md)

The destination is an **open-ended laboratory that discovers unexpected goals
and competencies across diverse systems**. The experiments below test pieces
of that process; none substitutes its benchmark for the destination.

We have reproduced a specific sorting phenomenon, inferred hidden authored targets
under constrained observation contracts, and tested several representations,
controls, engines, and visual instruments. **We have not demonstrated an
open-ended procedure discovering unexpected goals across diverse systems.**
The immediate research gap is candidate proposal followed by a distinguishing
intervention—not another generic simulator or representation leaderboard.

## How to read this page

This page owns the current cross-experiment interpretation. Native protocols and
results own exact hypotheses, measurements, and historical verdicts. The
[structured register](experiments.json) owns inventory metadata; its
[readable view](experiments.md) groups protocols/results by experiment rather
than making each Markdown file appear to be a separate experiment.

This synthesis reviews selected result/decision prose, not every protocol, raw
run, or executable. Register entries marked `not_reviewed` have no inferred
verdict. `result_reviewed` is not an independent reproduction. P8/P9 and the
optional composition preview are integrated in this revision; their software
verification does not revalidate archived science. The current plan owns
integration checks and installation status.

Provenance also has limits: the [post-002 checkpoint](../goal-discovery/docs/audits/2026-08-29_post_002_evidence_checkpoint.md)
records that raw generated data were ignored rather than versioned, and that
the checkpoint cannot retroactively prove every later protocol preceded its
already-generated data. Treat those studies as documented internal results with
their stated confidence limits, not externally timestamped preregistrations.
New promoted predictions require a committed protocol before the held-out batch.

Corrections and later confirmation outrank an earlier attractive headline.
A pass applies to its declared question; a stop applies to its experiment or
research route. Neither silently changes the charter.

## 1. Sorting: replication, opportunity, and mechanism

**Question:** Can the apparatus reproduce a published phenomenon and distinguish
an unreachable target from failure to exploit an available route?

[001 confirmation](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/001_sorting_confirmation_results.md)
reproduced the published bubble/selection freezing ranking inversion.
Immovable-freeze numerical agreement was close, but moveable-freeze errors were
systematically lower: directional replication, only partial quantitative
replication. Selection's weak late recovery also exposed a distinction between
achieving order once and retaining the ability to restore it.

The same record withdrew the R7 hidden-state ordering claim: changing the
matched-point convention changed the conclusion. Its C2 mechanism-damage
headline was subsequently narrowed by the passive bowl control below. Do not
reuse the stronger historical language without those corrections.

[P2-004](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p2_004_moveable_order_reachability_results.md)
classified all 90,720 new size-6 bubble-rule cases using passive-cell order.
[P2-005](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p2_005_opportunity_adjusted_performance_results.md)
found that algotypes differed mainly in reachable opportunity; conditional
route-realization differences missed the promotion gate.

**Implication:** retain the distinction between unreachable,
reachable-but-unrealized, and robust recovery. The historical mechanical sorting
line closed; that is not a ban on using sorting for a separately scoped discovery
calibration. The next useful question is whether a proposal procedure identifies
a testable pattern rather than restating supplied sorting metrics.

## 2. Calibration: convergence, defense, compensation, adaptation

**Question:** Can our instrument distinguish stronger interpretations from
simpler dynamical explanations?

[002 bowl](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/002_bowl_results.md) reproduced 001's
mechanism-damage signature in a passive system. That signature detects omitted
mechanism variables, not active agency. Its failed persistence criterion also
showed that first entry into a tolerance region is not settling.

[003B](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/003b_blind_target_inference_results.md)
inferred the hidden scalar target in both active and passive trajectories.
Held-out sustained loads and sensing/actuation ablations supplied the missing
contrast: only intact feedback strongly defended it.
[004](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/004_compensation_results.md) verified
engineered compensation through alternate routes;
[005](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/005_adaptation_results.md) verified
history-dependent parameter improvement that disappeared when memory was reset.

**Limit:** variables, candidate families, challenges, routing, and learning rules
were supplied. These are useful calibrated controls, not open-ended discovery.
Carry the separation between *inferring a candidate* and *attributing its defense*
into the next proposal test; do not optimize already-passing demonstrations.

## 3. Representation: useful descriptions must beat simple explanations

**Question:** Which representations add reliable predictive information?

[P2-002](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p2_002_distributed_macro_prediction_results.md)
found a promising capability-aware predictor, but missed its frozen gate.
[P5-000](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p5_000_representation_discovery_benchmark_results.md)
rejected promotion of the generic time-series pipeline: repeated pre-damage
scalar histories lacked later relational information, while the thermostat
result was fragile to top-feature removal and had few independent holdouts.

[P7-002](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p7_002_prospective_network_selector_results.md)
missed untouched confirmation despite a discovery win. Its stronger ablation
could not be promoted after opening confirmation.
[P7-003](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p7_003_selector_complexity_audit_results.md)
then found that matching feature counts did not make generic family selection
stable; that retrospective audit retired the generic selector.

**Implication:** observation content, independent variation, and question-specific
nulls matter more than feature volume. Prefer a small justified representation
for a concrete prediction/intervention question. Do not repair opened
confirmation or resurrect the leaderboard. A failed macro-prediction route does
not prohibit all elementary competency investigations.

## 4. Reused generators and visual instruments

**Question:** Can mature engines expose informative dynamics without building
our own simulator—and what do their pictures actually establish?

- [Flocking adoption](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p3_001_flocking_spike_results.md)
  worked, but [discrimination](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p3_002_flocking_representation_discrimination_results.md)
  separated local alignment from unstable global organization and found no
  defense of prior absolute heading.
- [Fireflies](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p3_003_fireflies_spike_results.md)
  lacked sufficient baseline synchrony and interpretable recovery in the tested
  default configuration.
- [Slime bidirectional test](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p3_006_slime_bidirectional_target_results.md)
  rejected a defended aggregation band; earlier interaction-dependent
  reconstruction did not establish setpoint regulation.
- [Heatbugs](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p4_002_heatbugs_blind_target_inference_results.md)
  recovered hidden heterogeneous individual targets and predicted held-out
  movement. Those were authored micro-targets, not an emergent collective goal.
- [Slime network extraction](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p5_001_slime_mold_network_threshold_results.md)
  worked as measurement infrastructure, but prediction failed.
  [Fresh confirmation](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p5_002_stability_plasticity_confirmation_results.md)
  failed the exploratory stability–plasticity claim, which was withdrawn.
- [Ants P7-004](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p7_004_ants_trail_scale_results.md)
  failed intervention integrity. Its prediction scores are diagnostic only,
  not a valid verdict against trail-scale predictive or causal value.
- [Network action P7-005](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p7_005_network_intervention_value_results.md)
  reused already-opened trajectories to compare degree-informed with random
  immunization at matched budgets. The reported 15.7% pooled burden reduction
  cleared its average-effect gate, but wins were only 10/16 and 9/16 seeds, and
  effects were positive in only 2/4 split-budget cells. Its retrospective
  robustness no-go does not establish prospective transfer or refute all
  network-scale actions. This integration reviewed the protocol and result;
  it did not independently rerun the original causal audit.

**Implication:** retain useful export, field/network, and visual adapters without
promoting the claims they helped reject. Reopen a generator for a distinct
decision-changing question, not its animation appeal. A visual view must expose
the contrast and its limitations, not simply display organized behavior.

## 5. Biological systems: evidence acquisition is distinct from engine adoption

**Question:** Does an available biological recovery package support a usable
independent ensemble and matched causal control?

[Neuromast P6-001](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/p6_001_neuromast_causal_calibration_results.md)
showed informative model-internal causal differences, but one of four active
seeds failed and mechanism-off runs became computationally unbounded. Eight of
twelve trajectories reached the planned endpoint; the no-go retained partial
controls rather than pretending the fixed-endpoint comparison completed.

[Archive qualification](../goal-discovery/docs/plans/p6_002_archive_first_benchmark_contract.md)
and [compact evidence acquisition](../goal-discovery/docs/plans/p6_003_compact_evidence_package_acquisition.md)
found no package meeting that route's declared contract. The missing interface
identified independent units, observable recovery outcomes, and matched or
properly censored controls—not merely simulator features or mean figures.

**Implication:** a convincing sample image or published mean is not a recovery
denominator. The historical biological macro-benchmark route stopped; its
specific qualification thresholds are not universal laboratory policy, and
external package acquisition is not a project-wide prerequisite.
Revisit when a concrete discovery question needs this evidence and the
acquisition cost is justified.

## 6. Composition: substrate support must earn its cost

**Question:** Does categorical execution add practical value beyond ordinary
validated Python while preserving behavior?

[Executable composition](../goal-discovery/docs/hypotheses/composition_exploration_results.md)
matched 34,992 state triplets across 144 configurations using native, validated
Python, and DisCoPy paths. Sorting and passive bowl reused the machinery.
Fidelity and finite interface checks passed; the practical value gate did not.
A type-correct no-op also demonstrated that valid wiring is not scientific validity.

**Limit:** the pilot composed whole-world steps, not interacting cell games or
full open-game semantics. Environments were prescribed, not reciprocally evolving.
No goal was discovered.

**Implication:** retain explicit interfaces and the optional prototype; defer a
production categorical runtime. Reconsider only when a real experiment exposes
an unmet composition need and a measurable advantage over ordinary code.

## Learning and drift review

These records show useful narrowing and abstention, but they do not establish
that our overall allocation was optimal. Comparable effort and time-to-decision
measurements have not been extracted; unknown cost must not be treated as zero.

At a checkpoint ask:

1. What belief or next decision changed, and which evidence changed it?
2. Did the work test a live alternative or merely make a known result prettier?
3. Is an apparent failure scientific, measurement-related, integrity-related,
   or a package/implementation limitation?
4. Are we reopening a closed route without new justification, or treating a
   historical route's gate as a universal prerequisite?
5. Which smallest next experiment could most change our decision at reasonable cost?

Repeated instrument failures suggest checking observation and intervention
contracts early. Repeated generic representation failures suggest a concrete
question before feature expansion. Successful tooling without a new scientific
decision should remain enabling infrastructure, not discovery progress.
The [current plan](../goal-discovery/docs/plans/current_research_plan.md) alone
selects the next action; this landscape is not a second task queue.
