## What it is, corrected

Its former `INTENT.md` described this as *"an external, independent research
thread — a separate ChatGPT-based conversation"* with `specimen_origin:
constructed/imported`. **That framing was wrong.** The owner's disposition,
2026-09-05: *"the ingression thread was a conceptual discussion to help the
collective competence repo."* It is not an import from elsewhere; it is this
project's own thinking, done in another window. It belongs here, it does not
expire, and it is not `levin-wiki` material — that repository is a wiki over
Levin's corpus, and this is not Levin's corpus.

## What is in here

**`narrative/`** — six documents, 5,496 lines, the conceptual discussion itself.
Until 2026-09-05 these existed in exactly one untracked copy at
`~/code/algorithmic_ingress*.md`, on a directory that is not a repository; see
[failure log F14](../../wiki/failure-log.md).

**The CSV/PNG/JSON files** — output from a 5,832-machine toy-automaton universe:
static versus dynamic occupancy of abstract patterns, drive-dependent selection
under periodic forcing, computational-capacity boundaries, and
representation-scrambling controls.

## What it contributed, and it is load-bearing on the current design

[The substrate design discussion](../../wiki/substrate-design.md) rests on two
results from `narrative/part5.md`:

> *drive can stabilize available structure, but it cannot stabilize a perfect
> structure the interface cannot implement*

and the decomposition *dynamic prevalence ≈ basin size × mechanical lifetime ×
coupling to the drive*, together with the computational-capacity boundary
(`3-state DFA → one counter` on Dyck-1; `pushdown → two counters` on aⁿbⁿcⁿ)
whose data is in `computational_capacity_ladder_summary.csv` and
`abc_capacity_boundary.csv`.

## What it is not — and this does not change

**The data is design input, not evidence.** No runnable source for it exists in
this repository. The generating agent's own `narrative/part5.md` states its
headline enrichment figures *"should not yet be treated as benchmark results"*,
and the positive controls it proposed were never run. Its own stated limitation:
the constitutive law — output/drive mismatch creates strain, strain increases
wiring failure — is a chosen toy physics, and the control asking whether the
phenomenon survives other energy laws and natural recodings has not been run.

Being this project's own conceptual work makes it **ours**. It does not make it
**verified**. Cite it for reasoning, never as a result.

## Relationship to `levin-wiki`

`levin-wiki` is a corpus wiki over Michael Levin's bibliography and should hold
Levin material only. It is a **reference source this project cites**, not a
member of this project. Its `platonic-space-and-ingression` page is an
independent treatment of the same concept and is worth reading alongside this;
neither owns the other.
